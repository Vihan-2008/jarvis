JARVIS for Mac
==============

1. Double-click "Install JARVIS".
   A Terminal window opens and asks a few questions. Press Return to accept each suggestion.

   If macOS says it cannot be opened ("Apple could not verify..." or "unidentified developer"):
   JARVIS is free and open source, and is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Install JARVIS" was blocked,
   click "Open Anyway" and confirm. (On older macOS: Control-click "Install JARVIS" and choose Open.)

2. When it has finished, open JARVIS from your Applications folder (or Launchpad, or Spotlight).
   The first time, macOS asks whether JARVIS may use the microphone and your Documents folder: allow both.

JARVIS needs Python 3.11 or newer. If it is missing, the installer says where to get it
(python.org, or "brew install python") and you run "Install JARVIS" again.

To remove JARVIS later, run uninstall.sh in its install folder (the installer says where).
Your notes and settings are kept.

The app's own files are in a hidden folder beside this note. You do not need to open it.
More: https://github.com/Vihan-2008/jarvis/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md
