JARVIS for Mac
==============

1. Double-click "Install JARVIS", then click Install.
   It takes about a minute. Nothing else needs to be installed: JARVIS brings everything it needs.

   If macOS says "Install JARVIS" cannot be opened ("Apple could not verify..." or "unidentified developer"):
   JARVIS is free and open source, and is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
   Click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about "Install JARVIS",
   click "Open Anyway" and confirm. Then double-click "Install JARVIS" again.
   (On macOS 14 or older you can instead Control-click "Install JARVIS" and choose Open.)

2. JARVIS opens by itself when it is installed. Next time, open it from Applications (or Launchpad, or Spotlight).
   Try your first command: type "open calculator" and press Return.

3. When macOS asks whether JARVIS may use the microphone, or control your computer, choose Allow.
   JARVIS asks only when it needs one, and its Settings show what is allowed.

To remove JARVIS later: in JARVIS, open Settings > Advanced and choose "Uninstall JARVIS".
Your notes are kept unless you choose to delete them.

More help: https://github.com/Vihan-2008/jarvis/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md
